About MTGForKids

Cards can do some good.

MTGForKids is a community fundraising effort built around Magic: The Gathering. Our events bring players and supporters together while raising money through Extra Life for Children's Miracle Network at Freeman Health System, helping kids and families in our local community.

You don't have to play in the tournament to help. Donations made through the official Extra Life campaign directly support the fundraiser.
