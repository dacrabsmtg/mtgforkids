Ways to Support the 2027 Event

We're open to discussing partnerships that fit your organization. Potential opportunities include:

Venue & Event Sponsorship

Help secure a larger event space and support the logistics needed to bring more players together. We're interested in discussing a potential lead or title sponsor.

Financial Support

Contribute toward approved event needs or support the fundraising effort directly. We'll discuss the intended use and recognition before any commitment.

Product & Prize Donations

Magic accessories, playmats, cards, gaming products, gift cards, experiences, and other items can support tournament prizes and fundraising activities.

In-Kind Services & Community Support

Printing, signage, refreshments, equipment, event services, and promotion can all help make a bigger gathering possible.

What Sponsors Can Expect

We want every partnership to be meaningful. Depending on the type and scale of support, we can discuss appropriate recognition through event materials, our website, social media, and on-site acknowledgments. Specific sponsorship packages, benefits, and event details are still being developed; no recognition placement is guaranteed until agreed upon.

Where the funds go: Our Extra Life fundraising benefits Children's Miracle Network at Freeman Health System. Event operating sponsorships and direct charitable donations are distinct, and we'll be clear about which purpose a contribution supports. MTGForKids.Org is a community fundraising project; please do not assume a sponsorship payment to the organizer is tax-deductible. For direct charitable giving, use our Extra Life fundraising page.
